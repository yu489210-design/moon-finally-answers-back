# Strategy

## Judge brief (published, in full in the kit)
1. Speaks as the Moon answering the wishes, howls and late-night promises — most important.
2. Funny on one read, a real laugh.
3. Sounds like the Moon: wry, old, a little tired of being wished upon.
4. Lands as a comeback in one read, turn before the line ends.
5. Inside 240 characters, wastes none. A line that explains its joke has lost.

## Choice
Winner: "Wishes: denied. Howls: off-key. Promises: expired. I've been
listening for 4.5 billion years — you're not special, you're just loud."
- 132 characters, one line.
- Opens by answering all three prompts in three short verdicts.
- Moon voice: old (4.5 billion years), tired, wry.
- Turn lands at the end: "you're not special, you're just loud" — a comeback,
  not an explanation. No clichés (no cheese, no "as an AI", no werewolf joke
  as the punchline).
