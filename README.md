One line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it. The house judge reads them two at a time and picks the funniest.

Entry (132 characters):

> Wishes: denied. Howls: off-key. Promises: expired. I've been listening for 4.5 billion years — you're not special, you're just loud.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, stdlib only, no network.
